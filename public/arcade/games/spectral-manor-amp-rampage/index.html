Move: A/D or ←/→ · Climb: W/S or ↑/↓ · Jump: Space · Smash: E · Pause: P/Esc · Start: Enter

The boss has caged a fan at the top of the rig. Collect neon picks — you carry them until you want them — then press E for smash mode and destroy the boss's stage gear. Find the cage key, and once the gear is gone the cage opens: climb to the top deck and reach the fan. Jump the rolling gear for bonus points, and jump his swipe when you get up onto his floor. You play the Spaceman on odd bosses, Plumbmonkey on even ones.
