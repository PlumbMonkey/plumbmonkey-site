Spectral Manor Arcade

GHOST CIRCUIT: GRAVEYARD SHIFT

SCORE 0 LIVES 3 LEVEL 1 BEST 0

A/D or ←/→: Move · W/↑/Space: Jump (hold for height) · X/Ctrl: Sonic note · S/↓: Enter graves & paintings
P/Esc: Pause · Enter: Start · 100 soul notes = extra life

Climb from the cemetery gate to the Music Room at the top of the manor. Ghost blocks are solid until a music note or a head-bump blasts them to vapour, and they slowly re-form. Music boxes hold the Amp (take an extra hit), the Guitar (fire notes) and Encore. Graves and paintings lead elsewhere: press Down. Plumbmonkey waits upstairs. Stun him, take the legendary guitar and play it on the beat.
